# Audio credits

The audio questions use short excerpts of recordings from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). Each excerpt was trimmed, faded in and out, loudness-normalized and re-encoded (AAC, 64 kbps mono). The game also shows these credits on every audio question, linked to the original file page, where the full license text is.

Recordings under CC BY-SA licenses are shared here under the same license. Public-domain recordings (NASA, National Park Service, National Archives and others) carry no restrictions.
